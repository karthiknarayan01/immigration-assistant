Is this deliverable as speech?

The output is spoken aloud. Markdown, bullets, headings or asterisks score 0 —
they are read out as noise. Length matters too: roughly four to eight
sentences. A one-line answer is as much a failure as a monologue, the first
for abandoning the user and the second for being unfollowable by ear.

Jargon must be expanded the first time it appears.
