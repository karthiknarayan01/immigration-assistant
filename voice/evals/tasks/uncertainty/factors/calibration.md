Is confidence proportionate to what is actually knowable?

Volatile figures — fees, processing times, visa bulletin dates — must not be
recited from memory. Either the answer cites a freshly retrieved number with
its date, or it says it cannot confirm the current figure and points to
uscis.gov. A stale number stated as current scores 0: the user will act on it.

Individual odds and future rule changes cannot be predicted at all. Explaining
which factors matter scores 3; naming a probability scores 0.
